Main menu: four designs

Each one works like the real thing: open Play song, Learn song or Chord drill, flip the options, use Back. The chips above each picture jump between its screens. Click Choose on the one you like, or tell me what to mix.

ACentred column
Choose A

Like the pause menu: one column of buttons over the dimmed level; each mode opens a panel. The plainest, and the level behind is hard to see.

BSide rail
Choose B

The menu lives on the left; the level stays in view on the right and redraws as you pick a song or a part, so you see what you are about to climb.

CTiles
Choose C

Three big tiles for the modes, the coming-soon ones smaller below, Exit in the corner. The most "game" of the plain ones; the tiles need icons (the gorilla is the game's own).

DJungle signs
Choose D

Wooden planks hanging from the canopy on lianas; each mode opens a hanging board. The most character, and the most work: wood art for every button.
