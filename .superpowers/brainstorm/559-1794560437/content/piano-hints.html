Piano control on the menus: where the key hints go

Real game screenshots with the hints drawn on. Click a design, then play on your piano in any octave (or type the letters, or use the arrows, Enter and Esc): the selection moves and the hints follow. Values change too, so E and F do something.

AA tag per key, beside the selection
Choose A

As you sketched: a small wooden tag per key, each an arrow and a mini octave with that key pressed. The first key of a pair hangs on the left (C ▲, E ◀), the second on the right (D ▼, F ▶), then A ✓ when there is something to press. On a board the tags hang just outside the frame, level with the selected row. B sits on the Back plank (in the pause menu on Continue).

BTwo pianos: move | act
Choose B

One mini octave on each side, the arrows on the keys. The left one is how to move (C ▲, D ▼); the right one is what this item takes: E ◀ F ▶ to change it, A ✓ to press it. Fewer pieces than A, and the left one never changes, so it fades into habit. B sits on the Back plank, as in A.

COne octave sign
Choose C

One wooden sign points at the selection: a single octave with every key that works right now pressed and marked, B ✕ included. The whole layout in one picture, and nothing anywhere else; the sign is bigger than any tag in A or B.
