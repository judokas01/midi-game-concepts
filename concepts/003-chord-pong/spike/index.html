Chord Pong

A creature at the top keeps throwing an elemental ball at you. Every element has its chord: fire = C, ice = Em, earth = G, water = Am.

Play the matching chord before the ball reaches the bottom and your shield sends it back. Any octave, any inversion. The last chord you played is your shield, and an extra wrong note spoils it.

Every return hurts the creature and speeds the ball up. Miss, and you lose a heart and the ball slows down.

No MIDI? Keys W E R T = C, Em, G, Am.
