King Gong

Swing the gorilla through the treetops. The five long bushes are the staff lines; every branch sits on a note. Play that note to grab the branch.

Hold the note as long as the branch is thick: thin = 1 beat, thick = 2 beats. Let go too early and you drop, hang on too long and the branch breaks. A liana below a gap is a rest: let go on time and bounce off it.

A wrong note is a fall too. Magic bananas bounce you back up. You start with 3, and more hang from some branches.

There is no backing track: the song starts when you grab the first branch and every grab restarts the beat.

No MIDI? Keys A S D F G H J K = C4 D4 E4 F4 G4 A4 B4 C5.
