# King Gong: song check

Tick a song once it sounds right. Under it, write what's wrong: the bar number and what you hear
("bar 5: should go up to G"). Play one from `godot-implementations/king-gong`:

```powershell
.\play-song 57          # song 57, tune and chords at 80 BPM, bar by bar
.\play-song 57 80 tune  # the tune alone
.\play-song list        # every song's number
```

**new** marks a song added on 2026-11-21. **score**: copied from a written public-domain score
(its link is in the comment above the song in `scripts/songbook/`). **memory**: written from
memory, so check every bar. The line under a song says what its writer was unsure of.
